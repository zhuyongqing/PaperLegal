FirstPass · Read it before you sign it

FirstPass reads a contract on your device and lists the clauses it finds, with the exact quote and page for each one.

Getting started

  1. Open Models and choose a model, or use Apple Intelligence if it is available on your device.
  2. Add a PDF, Word document, scan or photo from the document list.
  3. Run a clause check, or switch to Extract to pull out money, dates, parties, obligations or your own request.
  4. Tap any finding to see the exact wording on its page.

No model yet? Tap See a sample review on the empty document list to see the output before downloading anything.

Models and Apple Intelligence

  • On iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence available, FirstPass uses Apple's on-device system model and needs no model download.
  • On other devices, choose a model in Models. The download can be several gigabytes; use Wi-Fi.
  • A larger model generally finds more clauses but takes longer. If a run is slow, try a smaller model or a shorter document.
  • Models are stored in the app's Documents/Models folder and can be deleted from Models at any time.

Privacy

The document is read on the device. Nothing is uploaded to FirstPass. See the Privacy Policy.

Not legal advice

FirstPass is a reading aid. It does not score risk, tell you whether to sign, or replace a lawyer. Check every quote against the page shown; a small on-device model can miss a clause or misread a scan.
