Privacy Policy

FirstPass · Last updated 25 November 2026

FirstPass reads contracts and other documents on your own device. There is no account to create and no FirstPass server that receives your documents.

The short version

  • Your document, its text, the findings and the questions you ask stay in the app's container on this device.
  • FirstPass does not upload, transmit, sell or share your document.
  • There is no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking in the app.
  • Text recognition, clause extraction, summaries and questions run on the device. Nothing is sent to a FirstPass server.

What is stored on the device

The app stores the document text it extracted, page numbers, review results, question history and your settings in its own app container. That data is used only to show the app back to you. It is excluded from iCloud backup.

You can remove a document and its review from the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its container and everything in it.

Camera, photos and files

FirstPass asks for camera or photo-library access only when you choose to photograph or import a document. The image is read on the device. It is not uploaded to FirstPass or to a third party.

On-device models

On iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence available, FirstPass uses Apple's on-device Foundation Model. Apple's system model is managed by iOS and runs on the device.

On other devices, FirstPass downloads a model file from Hugging Face (or another model host you choose). That download is a normal network request to the host's CDN; it does not include your document. The downloaded model runs entirely on the device.

What FirstPass does not do

  • No account, sign-in or email address is required.
  • No document is uploaded, indexed or stored on a server.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting SDK or tracking pixels.
  • No access to contacts, calendar, location or health data.

Children

FirstPass is a general-purpose reading tool. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
