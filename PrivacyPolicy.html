Privacy Policy

Paper · Last updated 23 October 2026

Paper is a checkbook register that keeps your ledger on your own device. There is no account to create, no bank to connect, and no advertising or analytics inside the app.

The short version

  • We do not collect, transmit, sell or share your ledger. We never see it.
  • Paper has no servers. Your accounts, entries, snapshots and receipt photos live in the app's own container on this device.
  • Nothing leaves the device unless you turn on iCloud sync or export a file yourself.

What Paper stores, and where

  • Your ledger — accounts, entries, categories, recurring rules and reconciliation records — in a database inside the app container.
  • Snapshots — copies of that database, kept beside it so any earlier state can be restored.
  • Receipt photos — only for the entries you attach one to, stored as files in the app container.
  • Preferences — lock, appearance, reminders, sync — in the app's local settings.
  • Widget snapshot — a small read-only summary written to a shared container so the home-screen widget can show totals without opening the database.

None of the above is sent anywhere by Paper.

What Paper does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address.
  • No connection to a bank, card or brokerage. Nothing is imported automatically: you type your entries, or import a CSV or QIF file you choose yourself.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking components.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information. There is nothing to sell.
  • No access to your contacts, calendar, location or health data.

Optional features that touch your data

iCloud sync (off by default)

If you turn it on, Paper stores your ledger in your own private iCloud database, under your Apple ID. Apple holds that data; we have no server and no way to read it. Turning sync off stops new changes from syncing. Deleting the app does not delete what is already in iCloud — manage that in Settings → your name → iCloud.

Face ID or passcode lock (off by default)

The lock is checked by iOS. Paper receives only a yes or a no; your biometric data never leaves the device and is never available to the app.

Reminders (off by default)

Reminders are scheduled by iOS on this device. Paper uses no push service, and no reminder content is sent to us.

Receipt photos

Paper asks for camera or photo-library access only at the moment you attach a receipt. The image is copied into the app container, so it travels with your snapshots and exports and is removed when the entry is.

Home-screen widget

The widget reads the small summary described above. It never opens the database and never writes to it.

Exports and backups

CSV and QIF files are created on the device and handed to the share sheet, where you choose the destination. Where that file goes is your decision.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Deleting an entry or account is a soft delete for 90 days, so a mistake can be undone. After that window Paper removes it during a launch, right after taking a snapshot.
  • Erase all data in Settings → Data clears the ledger immediately (a snapshot is taken first) and also clears saved searches and drafts.
  • Deleting the app removes everything in its container, including snapshots and receipt photos.
  • If you enabled iCloud sync, remove the iCloud copy from your Apple ID settings as well.

Children

Paper is not directed at children under 13, and it collects nothing from anyone — including children.

Your rights

Data-protection rules give you the right to see, correct, export and delete the personal data an app holds about you. Paper holds none on our side, and every one of those rights is available to you directly in the app: the register shows and edits your entries, Data & backup exports them, and Erase all data deletes them. If you believe we hold something about you, write to the address below.

US state privacy rights

If you live in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas or another US state with a consumer privacy statute, those laws give residents the right to know what personal information a business holds, to delete and correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. Paper has nothing to disclose: it collects no personal information, runs no analytics or advertising, and does not track you across apps or websites. There is no data to sell, share or correct, and no opt-out to exercise. Everything the app knows stays in its own container on your device, and Settings → Data erases it in one tap.

Third parties

Paper relies on Apple services only: the App Store for downloads, iCloud for optional sync, iOS for notifications and for the lock. Apple's own privacy policy covers those. No other company receives anything from Paper.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version ships in the app. Continuing to use Paper after that means you accept the updated policy.
